Corporate History

Architecting Superior Environments with Local Precision

Established in Phnom Penh, SDY Company C&I has pioneered high-precision engineering and bespoke interior fit-out contracting. Our unified capability—operating local high-output steel mills and timber joinery plants—allows us to eliminate middle-man costs and deliver flawless quality directly.

To construct safer, sustainable, and highly sophisticated environments using state-of-the-art local manufacturing methods.
